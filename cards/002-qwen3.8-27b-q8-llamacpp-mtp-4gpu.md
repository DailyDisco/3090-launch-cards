## Metric

Throughput is end-to-end output tokens per second per user, including prefill and queue wait. Workload from SUMMARY section 2: 2,400-token prompts, 256-token answers, 90-second window.

That is not `median_decode_tokens_per_s`. The SUMMARY says that field measures the decode phase only, excludes queue time, and reads far higher (its example is 103 tokens/s at 16 clients). It does not describe what a user experiences. This card does not report that field.

Prefill tok/s in the older sense (input tokens / mean TTFT) was not measured in the Nov 1 protocol.

Decode tok/s in the older sense (1000 / mean TPOT, one stream) was not measured in the Nov 1 protocol.

Mean TTFT in milliseconds was not measured in the Nov 1 protocol. results.json does record `median_ttft_s` (seconds) on `speed.qwen27b_q8_4gpu`. Those values are in the table below.
